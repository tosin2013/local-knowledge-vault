# Cell structure (Unit 2)

ok so basically there are two kinds of cells. **Prokaryotes** (bacteria, archaea) have no nucleus and no membrane-bound organelles; their DNA sits in a region called the nucleoid. **Eukaryotes** (plants, animals, fungi, protists) have a nucleus and organelles.

Organelles I need to know:
- Nucleus: holds DNA, control center. Nucleolus inside makes ribosomes.
- Ribosomes: make proteins. Free in cytoplasm or stuck on rough ER.
- Rough ER: has ribosomes, makes/modifies proteins. Smooth ER: lipids, detox.
- Golgi apparatus: packages and ships proteins (the "post office").
- Mitochondria: cellular respiration, makes ATP. Has its own DNA (endosymbiosis!).
- Chloroplasts: photosynthesis, plants only. Also own DNA.
- Lysosomes: digestive enzymes, mostly animal cells.
- Vacuole: storage; plants have one big central vacuole that keeps the cell turgid.
- Cell membrane: phospholipid bilayer, selectively permeable.
- Cell wall: plants (cellulose), fungi (chitin), bacteria (peptidoglycan). NOT animals.

Plant vs animal: plant has cell wall, chloroplasts, big central vacuole. Animal has centrioles and lysosomes (usually), no wall.
