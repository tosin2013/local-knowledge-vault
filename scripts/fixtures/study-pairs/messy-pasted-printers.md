Printer Troubleshooting for A+ (Updated!!)   ★★★★☆ 4.2 (318 reviews)
Share: Facebook Twitter LinkedIn
Laser printer imaging process — 7 steps: Processing, Charging, Exposing, Developing, Transferring, Fusing, Cleaning.
Tip: "People Can't Eat Dry Toast For Christmas"
Faded prints -> low toner or toner saver mode.    Ghost images -> drum/imaging problem or cleaning blade.
Toner smears / rubs off -> FUSER not hot enough!!! (fuser = heat + pressure)
Paper jams -> check pickup rollers, separation pad, right paper weight
Garbled characters -> wrong/corrupt print driver
Inkjet: clogged nozzle -> run cleaning cycle. Thermal printer: uses heat-sensitive paper, receipts. Impact printer: multipart forms, needs ribbon.
