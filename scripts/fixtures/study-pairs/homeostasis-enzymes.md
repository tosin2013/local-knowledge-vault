# Homeostasis + enzymes

Homeostasis = keeping a stable internal environment (temp, blood sugar, water balance). Uses feedback.
- Blood sugar high -> pancreas releases insulin -> cells take in glucose -> sugar goes down. (negative feedback)
- Guard cells open/close stomata to balance water loss and CO2 intake in plants.

Enzymes:
- proteins that speed up reactions (biological catalysts), not used up
- shape matters: active site fits the substrate (lock and key)
- temperature or pH too far from optimum -> enzyme denatures, loses shape, stops working
- human enzymes work best around 37 °C

Immune system: antibodies recognize antigens. Vaccines contain weakened/dead pathogens or parts so the body makes memory cells. Antibiotics kill bacteria, NOT viruses.
