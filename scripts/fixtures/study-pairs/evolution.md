# Evolution / natural selection

Darwin's idea, in my words:
1. Organisms produce more offspring than can survive.
2. There is variation (from mutation + sexual reproduction).
3. Some variations help survival in that environment (adaptations).
4. Those individuals survive and reproduce more, so the trait gets more common over generations.

Example: antibiotic resistance. The antibiotic doesn't *cause* the mutation — resistant bacteria were already there and they survive.

Evidence: fossils, comparative anatomy (homologous structures), DNA/protein similarities, embryology.

Extinction happens when a species can't adapt to environmental change fast enough.
